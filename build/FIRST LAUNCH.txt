DOWNY DOWNLOADER — FIRST LAUNCH
===============================

1. Drag "Downy Downloader" into the Applications folder.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Downy Downloader.
3. macOS may show "Apple could not verify..." because this build is ad-hoc signed
   rather than notarized with a paid Apple Developer certificate.

   If that happens:
   • macOS 15 Sequoia or newer:
     Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to Downy Downloader. Confirm, then open it again.
   • macOS 14 Sonoma or older:
     Right-click (or Control-click) Downy Downloader and choose Open.
     Confirm the dialog.

   You only need to do this once. After the first launch, the app opens normally.

INSTALL NOTES
-------------
• This is a universal app: it runs natively on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
• Requires macOS 12 Monterey or newer.
• No Python, Homebrew, ffmpeg, or yt-dlp installation is required. Everything is
  bundled inside the app.
• The app keeps its tools in ~/Library/Application Support/Downy Downloader.
• If YouTube changes something, open Settings → Advanced and click
  "Update yt-dlp". No new app download needed.

TOKENS
------
• The app uses download tokens.
• Each normal download costs 5 tokens.
• A complete playlist costs ceil(5 × number of videos ÷ 2).
  Examples: 3 videos = 8 tokens, 10 videos = 25 tokens.
• Failed or cancelled downloads refund automatically.
• Your balance is shown in the wallet in the top-right.
